Dataset Overview

data/household_electricity.csv

The dataset captures core daily electrical drivers across residential households. Below is the feature definition and preview of sample records.

1. People
Number of residents residing in the home (1–8)
2. AC Usage Hours
Daily air conditioning operating duration (Hours/day)
3. Number of Fans
Total ceiling/pedestal fans in regular daily use (1–8)
4. Electrical Appliances
Active heavy/medium appliances (Fridge, TV, PC, Washing machine)
5. Daily Usage Hours
Total active electricity consumption duration (Hours/day)
6. Outside Temperature
Average ambient outdoor temperature in °Celsius
7. Previous Consumption
Baseline reading from previous billing/monitoring day (kWh)
🎯 Target: Electricity Consumption
Actual Daily Household Power Consumption in kWh/day
